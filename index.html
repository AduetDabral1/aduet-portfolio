CoinKart – Cryptocurrency Portfolio Allocation & Optimization

Project Lead (4-Member Team) Modern Portfolio Theory Sharpe Ratio Optimization IBM Qiskit Quantum Algorithms Crypto Market API ETL

Project Overview & Technical Approach:
CoinKart was a final-year capstone project focused on cryptocurrency portfolio allocation and optimization. Led as project lead on a 4-member team, the project applied modern portfolio theory to the crypto market by implementing Sharpe Ratio calculations and Markowitz Portfolio Theory to identify risk-adjusted optimal asset allocations. The technical architecture included a complete ETL pipeline — data collection from crypto market APIs, standardization and validation of price feeds and market indicators, and transformation into analysis-ready datasets. Core analysis incorporated sentiment analysis of market conditions alongside quantitative metrics, with a novel application of Qiskit (IBM's quantum computing library) to model and optimize portfolio selection using quantum algorithms.

Delivery & Business Impact:
The project delivered a full-stack solution combining backend quantitative analysis with a user-facing frontend interface. Results included optimized portfolio recommendations ranked by Sharpe Ratio, Markowitz-efficient frontiers, and quantum-computed allocation strategies. The work demonstrated end-to-end capability in data pipeline design, statistical modeling, and emerging technology integration (quantum computing), providing practical insight into how traditional investment theory can be adapted and enhanced with advanced computational methods for the volatile cryptocurrency market.
